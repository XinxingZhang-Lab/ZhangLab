We are thrilled to share that the Zhang Lab has received research funding from the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to support our project, “Molecular and Cellular Mechanisms of Fluid Balance in C. elegans” (Grant No. 1R35GM162254-01), led by Dr. Xinxing Zhang.

This funding will support our efforts to uncover the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying fluid balance using Caenorhabditis elegans as a model organism, advancing our understanding of this fundamental biological process.

We are deeply grateful to the NIH for its generous support and recognition of our research. This award represents an exciting milestone for our lab and provides a wonderful opportunity to pursue new discoveries.
