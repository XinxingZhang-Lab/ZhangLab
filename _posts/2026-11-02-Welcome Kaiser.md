The Zhang Lab is excited to extend a belated but warm welcome to Kaiser! Kaiser is an academically outstanding high school student with a strong passion for science. This year, he joined our lab through a research program to gain hands-on experience in scientific research, exploring Caenorhabditis elegans (C. elegans) and the functions of its sensory neurons.

We are delighted to have Kaiser as part of our team and look forward to seeing his curiosity and enthusiasm for science grow. It is truly a pleasure to get to know this promising "future scientist"!
